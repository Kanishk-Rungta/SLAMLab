# Code of conduct

SLAMLab adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its community behavioral standard, including its enforcement guidelines.
The standard is authored by the Contributor Covenant community and licensed
under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Be respectful, welcome different experience levels, and critique ideas with
technical evidence. Harassment, discrimination and disclosure of private
information are unacceptable in project spaces.

Maintainers enforce this policy. Use a private reporting channel listed on a
maintainer's GitHub profile or GitHub's abuse-reporting interface. No project
email address or confidential reporting service is invented here.
