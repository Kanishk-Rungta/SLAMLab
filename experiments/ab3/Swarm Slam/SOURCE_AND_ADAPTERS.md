# Source and adapters

The [guide](../../../algorithms/swarm-slam/README.md) records upstream attribution and pin. The team adapter composes backend graph corrections with native VO and forwards native inter-robot messages through the standard radio. Raw RTAB-Map odometry is not labeled corrected Swarm output.

Original files are traced in the [import manifest](../../../docs/import-manifest.json). Fetched snapshots and model weights retain upstream licenses.
