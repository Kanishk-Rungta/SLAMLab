# AB3 scenario contract

The team's imported first-floor world is a simulation approximation, not an
as-built survey. Retained geometry, PROTOs, textures, calibration and occupancy
ground truth belong to one fixed standard. Their source hashes are in the
[import manifest](../import-manifest.json).

The world contains ten differential-drive simulated robots, numbered `bstem1`
through `bstem10`, at fixed poses. Stereo images are 320?360, camera period
96 ms, physics/control period 32 ms. Stereo baseline is 0.025 m. A simulator
sensor interface supplies encoders, gyro, accelerometer and radio with seeded
noise. Simulated communication uses channel 42, 40 m range, configured loss,
delay and per-robot bandwidth. These are simulator settings, not physical
measurements or guaranteed network behavior.

Use the [simulation index](../../simulations/webots/README.md) for commands. Native
SLAM adapters access the shared sensor model. Radio comparisons require every
peer message to traverse that model; a shared ROS master can bypass it.

The odometry example runs a gentle arc for a short period and outputs timestamp,
x/y metres and yaw radians. It uses a known initial dock and noisy wheel
integration. The separate supervisor records world-frame truth. Output files
stay under `outputs/`. No occupancy map or multi-robot graph is generated by this
example. Do not present its trajectories as a research SLAM result.

The imported evaluator accepts the original benchmark recorder format. Some
metrics are heuristic or model-based (for example energy estimates) and are not
measured electrical consumption. The focused trajectory CLI documents its own
validated scope separately. Matching world fingerprints are necessary but do
not remove sensor, exploration, scale or compute-budget confounds.

The historical generators `make_ab3_world.py` and `make_layout.py` were excluded:
they depended on absent archive profiles/controller configuration. The standard
world regeneration helper and reference world are retained. Do not regenerate a
standard silently; update and review its fingerprint after any intentional change.
